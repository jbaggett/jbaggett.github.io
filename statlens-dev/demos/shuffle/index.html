What does a shuffle show?

If the treatment does nothing, then each person’s outcome would have been the same whichever group they landed in — so the twelve cards could just as well have been dealt the other way. Each shuffle deals the same twelve outcomes into two new groups of six and measures the gap between them. The pile of those gaps is what chance alone produces.

There is no population here. Nothing is being sampled from anywhere — the only thing that changes between shuffles is which six people are called “treatment”. That is what makes this a randomization test.

With six in each group and seven improvements in all, the gap can only take six values: ±5/6, ±1/2 and ±1/6. The spikes are those six values, not bins.

Add ?observed=on to mark the observed gap of +0.50 and shade everything at least that far out.
