Normal Distribution Calculator

Computes areas (probabilities) under a normal curve. With the mean at 0 and SD at 1, the boundary is a z-score.

Region to shade

The buttons above the chart choose which area is shaded and reported:

  • Left — area below the boundary
  • Right — area above the boundary
  • Between — area in the middle band between two boundaries (a second input appears)
  • Symmetric — the two outer tails, mirrored about the mean

Two-way lookup

The calculator works in both directions — edit whichever value you know:

  • Type a boundary in the z score box to get the shaded area.
  • Type an area in the Probability box to get the boundary (the critical value) that produces it.

You can also drag the boundary line(s) directly on the chart. The banner beside the buttons reports the current area.

Distribution shape

Set Mean (μ) and SD (σ) to match your distribution. Leaving them at 0 and 1 gives the standard normal, so the boundary reads as a z-score.
