For instructors

Every tool is addressable by URL, so a slide, a textbook page or a homework problem can link a specific function, dataset or seed — and can strip the page down to just the figure for embedding. The parameters are documented and frozen: the URL API.

Nothing loads from a content delivery network. Charts, fonts and maths all ship with the tools, so a lecture does not depend on the room's wifi.
